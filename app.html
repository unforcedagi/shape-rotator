Two different objects, two different rotations, drawn independently — and at cue 0 these two canvases are identical, pixel for pixel, forever. That is the whole illusion, and it is an exact algebraic fact rather than a good approximation. Push the cue up and watch them come apart: the cue is the depth information the flat picture never had.
