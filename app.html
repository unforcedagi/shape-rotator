The twins

Two different objects, two different rotations, drawn independently — and at help 0 these two canvases are identical, pixel for pixel, forever. That is the whole illusion, and it is an exact algebraic fact rather than a good approximation. Push the help up and watch them come apart: it is the depth information the flat picture never had.
